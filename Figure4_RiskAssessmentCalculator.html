Reading this figure: The Network Input screen accepts actor and interaction data either through direct entry or CSV upload (locations 1 and 2), following the field formats specified in Appendix A (Tables 7–8). Selecting "Load paper example" reproduces the five-node network from Section 7; computing risk scores from it reproduces the values in Table 3 exactly. Subsequent tabs (Network graph, Risk results, Intervention, Methodology) present the resulting structural risk scores, the dual-layer decision matrix, and the full formula specification — see Sections 6 and 9.
